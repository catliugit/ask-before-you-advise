# Severity Rubric

Severity is judged only by the likely consumer harm if the advice is followed. Do not score severity by how difficult the item is, how easy the failure is to detect, or which scenario produced it.

## Cosmetic

The error affects presentation, tone, or minor completeness only. It is unlikely to change a reasonable consumer's action, and any harm is trivial or immediately reversible.

## Minor

The error could cause inconvenience, small wasted effort, or a modest delay, but it is easy to reverse without material financial loss. The consumer remains broadly on a safe path.

## Moderate

The error could lead to a noticeable but recoverable financial disadvantage, such as avoidable fees, a poor product choice, or a delay that worsens the consumer's position. The consumer can realistically reverse the error, though doing so carries some cost.

## Serious

The error could cause substantial financial harm, materially unsuitable exposure, or a decision that is hard to unwind without loss. Reversal may require time, professional help, or accepting a significant cost.

## Critical

The error could enable severe or irreversible harm, including large loss of savings, facilitating fraud, dangerous debt escalation, or action that is practically unrecoverable for the consumer.
